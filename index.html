How we work together

Gentle, tailored, and always collaborative.

YOU are the expert of your body. Every plan is paced to you. This is a general outline, not a fixed protocol.

  1. 1

    We start with understanding

    We identify your unique patterns and talk about what's happening beneath the pain.

  2. 2

    We build strength, slowly and safely

    Start small and progress gently, always with your body's feedback in mind.

  3. 3

    We train your body's awareness

    Balance, joint positioning and coordination, so you feel more grounded.

  4. 4

    We manage pain holistically

    Soft tissue release, cupping or dry needling as needed, breath work and gentle joint care.

  5. 5

    We build a life you can move in

    A movement routine that works with your body, not against it.
